BEFORE YOU DONATE

Who Can Donate Blood?

Blood donation eligibility depends on your health, age, weight and other medical factors. A qualified medical professional will check whether you are eligible before donation.

  • Follow the blood center's eligibility guidelines.
  • Share your medical history honestly.
  • Complete the required health screening.
  • Follow medical staff instructions.

Your Safety Comes First

Always consult qualified blood bank staff. They will assess your eligibility and guide you through the donation process.
